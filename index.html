KeepMemo

Privacy Policy — Last updated: August 2026

Overview

KeepMemo is a personal memory app designed to help you keep track of the people and moments that matter to you. Your privacy is our highest priority.

All data you enter into KeepMemo — including names, birthdays, notes, photos, and voice recordings — is stored only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share your data with any server, company, or third party.

What Data We Store

KeepMemo stores the following data locally on your device:

  • People profiles — names, photos, birthdays, notes, and relationships
  • Journal entries — text, mood, emotional tags, and linked people
  • Memories — photos, videos, voice recordings, captions, and emotional tags
  • Plans — titles, dates, reminders, and linked people
  • Gifts — gift ideas, given gifts, received gifts, and occasions
  • Important dates — anniversaries, first meetings, memorials, and celebrations
  • Check-ins — contact tracking and last interaction dates
  • App settings — notification times, lock preferences, theme, and assistant settings

None of this data leaves your device unless you choose to export a backup file yourself.

Memory Assistant (AI Features)

KeepMemo includes an optional Memory Assistant feature that uses on-device artificial intelligence to help you search, summarize, and explore your memories. Here's how it works:

  • On-Device Processing — The AI model runs entirely on your device. Your data never leaves your phone or tablet for AI processing.
  • Model Download — When you first enable the Memory Assistant, you download an AI model (~400MB) to your device. This is a one-time download.
  • No Cloud API — The Assistant does not use any cloud-based AI services. All processing happens locally using the downloaded model.
  • Your Data Only — The Assistant only works with data you've entered into KeepMemo. It cannot access information from other apps or the internet.
  • Optional Feature — The Memory Assistant is completely optional. You can use KeepMemo without ever enabling it.

The AI model is stored in your device's application storage and can be deleted at any time by disabling the Memory Assistant in Settings.

Permissions We Request

  • Camera & Photo Library — to let you add photos and videos to memories and people profiles. We never access your gallery without your action.
  • Microphone — to record voice notes attached to memories. Recordings are saved only on your device.
  • Notifications — to send you reminders for birthdays, anniversaries, plans, and check-ins. You can disable these at any time in Settings.
  • Storage — to let you export and import backup files, and to store the AI model if you enable the Memory Assistant. Backup files stay on your device or wherever you choose to save them.
  • Contacts (Optional) — to import people from your contacts into KeepMemo. This is entirely optional and requires your explicit permission. We never access your contacts without your action.

Backup Files

When you export a backup, KeepMemo creates a file on your device containing all your data including photos, voice recordings, and the AI model if you have it downloaded. This file is unencrypted. You are responsible for storing it securely. KeepMemo does not upload or access your backup files.

Cloud Sync (Optional)

KeepMemo may offer optional cloud backup/sync features in the future. If you choose to enable cloud sync:

  • End-to-End Encryption — Your data will be encrypted before it leaves your device. Only you can decrypt it.
  • Optional Feature — Cloud sync is completely optional. KeepMemo works perfectly without it.
  • Your Control — You can disable cloud sync at any time and delete your cloud data.

As of August 2026, cloud sync features are still in development. All data remains local-only unless explicitly stated otherwise.

No Analytics or Tracking

KeepMemo does not include any analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking tools. We do not know how you use the app, and we do not want to.

Children's Privacy

KeepMemo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be available within the app and at this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
